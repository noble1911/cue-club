# Sound credits

The room ambience recordings come from [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com), released under
**CC0 (public domain)**: free to use for any purpose, no attribution required. Credited anyway,
with thanks. Each was cut to a 60 s seamless loop (equal-power crossfade), loudness-matched and,
for the snooker club, muffled to sound like a far-off room.

| File | Room | Source recording |
|------|------|------------------|
| `club.mp3` | Snooker club | [Small Restaurant Conversations](https://bigsoundbank.com/small-restaurant-conversations-s3542.html) (s3542) |
| `pub.mp3` | Pub (UK 8-ball) | [Coffee Shop at the Capucins](https://bigsoundbank.com/coffee-shop-at-the-capucins-s2561.html) (s2561) |
| `hall.mp3` | Pool hall (US 8-ball, 9-ball) | [Hall of the Capuchins](https://bigsoundbank.com/hall-of-the-capuchins-s2562.html) (s2562) |

Every other sound in the game is synthesised in `src/audio.ts`.
